# Volume 3 / Act I Research — 2018~2020

## R3-A1-01 — 2018 Crypto Winter
- Reuters reported Bitcoin trading around $3,250 on 2018-12-13, down more than 80% from the prior December record high.
- Reuters reported Bitcoin around $4,000 on 2018-12-20 after having lost about half its value in the preceding six weeks.
- The broader 2017 ICO boom unwound sharply through 2018; later Reuters reporting described total crypto market value as having fallen roughly 85% from the early-2018 peak by Feb. 2019.
- Narrative rule: the winter is not one single-day crash. Treat it as a year-long loss of confidence, project failures, treasury pressure and infrastructure building.
- Sources: Reuters 2018-12-13, 2018-12-20; Reuters 2019-02-08.

## R3-A1-02 — Dai and collateralized stable money
- MakerDAO's original Single-Collateral Dai system launched on Ethereum mainnet on 2017-12-19.
- Users could generate Dai against ETH/PETH collateral through Collateralized Debt Positions (CDPs).
- The system used liquidation rules when collateralization became too risky.
- Multi-Collateral Dai later launched in Nov. 2019; do not project later collateral diversity backward into 2018.
- Sources: Maker/Sky official whitepaper and historical DeFi timeline.

## R3-A1-03 — USDC launch
- Circle and CENTRE launched USDC on 2018-09-26 as a fiat stablecoin pegged 1:1 to USD, with Circle as the first commercial issuer.
- At launch it was ERC-20 and had support from a broad group of exchanges, protocols, wallets and applications.
- Circle marketed reserves/attestation and redeemability as core characteristics.
- Narrative rule: USDC≠Circle. Stablecoin token, issuer and reserve system are distinct layers.
- Sources: Circle 2018-09-26 `Introducing USD Coin`; ecosystem launch announcement.

## R3-A1-04 — Compound
- Compound launched its Ethereum money-market protocol on 2018-09-27.
- The initial system let users lend or borrow supported crypto assets with dynamic interest rates rather than negotiate with a direct counterparty.
- Compound v2 followed in 2019; the precise v2 mechanics should not be projected into the 2018 scene unless separately checked.
- Source: Compound Finance 2018-09-27 launch post; contemporaneous TechCrunch coverage.

## R3-A1-05 — Uniswap
- Uniswap was publicly announced and deployed to Ethereum mainnet on 2018-11-02.
- Its early model used automated market-maker contracts and liquidity pools rather than a traditional order book.
- Uniswap v2 launched 2020-05-18, adding ERC20/ERC20 pairs, price oracles and flash swaps.
- Sources: Hayden Adams, `A Short History of Uniswap`; Uniswap Labs V2 launch post.

## R3-A1-06 — COMP distribution and DeFi Summer
- Compound began distributing COMP to protocol users on 2020-06-15 after governance approval.
- Users earned COMP while supplying/borrowing in supported markets. This incentive structure became a major template for liquidity mining/yield farming.
- Contemporary reporting and later research associate COMP distribution with rapid expansion of DeFi experimentation.
- Source: Compound community records; The Block 2020-06-15; academic research on Compound liquidations/governance.

## Act I 창작 경계
- `Winter`, `Stablecoins`, `Dai`, `USDC`, `Compound`, `Uniswap`, `Yield` may be personified. Dialogue is C2.
- Do not imply all stablecoins share the same backing mechanism. Fiat-reserve tokens and crypto-collateralized Dai are structurally different.
- Do not call DeFi lender smart contracts 'riskless banks'. Overcollateralization, oracle risk, liquidation risk, smart-contract risk and governance risk remain.
- Do not claim Uniswap invented every AMM concept. In the novel it represents the 2018 Ethereum AMM breakthrough/ecosystem, not the first mathematical AMM idea in history.
- `DeFi Summer` is a retrospective label for a period, not an official start date. COMP distribution on 2020-06-15 is used as a narrative ignition point, not as the sole cause.
