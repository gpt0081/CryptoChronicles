# Volume 3 / Act III Research — Terra, Credit Contagion, FTX, The Merge

## R3-A3-01 — UST/LUNA design and Anchor
- UST was marketed as an algorithmic stablecoin tied to LUNA rather than backed 1:1 by cash reserves. Court/SEC records describe a mechanism where 1 UST could be exchanged for $1 worth of LUNA and vice versa under the protocol design.
- Anchor Protocol marketed a high stablecoin yield, publicly targeting roughly 20% APR.
- Later court/SEC findings and litigation records also show that a May 2021 UST depeg was supported by substantial third-party purchases, so do not portray the system as having demonstrated purely autonomous self-healing in 2021.
- Sources: SEC v. Terraform Labs amended complaint and later court findings; SEC 2023/2024 releases.

## R3-A3-02 — May 2022 Terra collapse
- UST lost its $1 peg in May 2022 and fell dramatically; Reuters reported a drop to about $0.30 on May 11.
- As UST was exchanged/sold and LUNA supply expanded, UST and LUNA entered a reflexive collapse and both approached negligible prices. Later SEC/court records describe more than $40bn of market value being wiped out across the interconnected ecosystem.
- The Terra blockchain was halted on 2022-05-12 after severe LUNA inflation reduced the cost of governance attack; validators later resumed block production.
- A recovery plan created a new Terra blockchain; the original chain/token became Terra Classic/Luna Classic.
- Sources: Reuters May 11/25 2022; SEC Terraform records; contemporary Terra halt reporting.

## R3-A3-03 — Celsius withdrawal freeze and bankruptcy
- Celsius paused all withdrawals and transfers on 2022-06-13, citing extreme market conditions.
- Reuters described crypto lenders such as Celsius as having attracted depositors with high yields and lent tokens mostly to institutional investors, earning the spread.
- Celsius filed Chapter 11 on 2022-07-13. Bankruptcy filings later disclosed a roughly $1.19bn balance-sheet deficit and more than 100,000 creditors.
- Sources: Reuters 2022-06-13; Reuters 2022-07-13/15.

## R3-A3-04 — 3AC failures and creditor network
- 3AC failed margin calls in June 2022. Reuters reported BlockFi had liquidated at least part of its positions.
- Voyager disclosed a loan exposure of 15,250 BTC (then ~$324m) plus $350m USDC and issued a default notice on 2022-06-27.
- A BVI court ordered 3AC into liquidation on 2022-06-27; the liquidation became public on June 29.
- Genesis later disclosed exposure after 3AC failed a margin call, saying loans had weighted-average margin requirements over 80%.
- Sources: Reuters 2022-06-16/27/29/07-06; Voyager 2022-06-27 statement.

## R3-A3-05 — Voyager bankruptcy
- Voyager filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions on 2022-07-05.
- Voyager's restructuring announcement explicitly cited crypto-market contagion and 3AC's default as reasons for the action and said it had claims against 3AC exceeding $650m.
- Sources: Voyager 2022-07-05 restructuring release; Reuters 2022-07-06; bankruptcy petition.

## R3-A3-06 — FTX collapse chronology
- FTX was one of the world's largest and most prominent centralized crypto exchanges going into late 2022.
- Public scrutiny of FTX/Alameda finances triggered a rapid loss of confidence in early Nov. 2022. Binance signed a non-binding LOI to potentially acquire FTX on 2022-11-08, subject to due diligence; the deal did not proceed.
- FTX Trading, FTX US, Alameda Research and roughly 130 affiliated companies commenced Chapter 11 proceedings on 2022-11-11; Sam Bankman-Fried resigned and John J. Ray III became CEO.
- Ray's later first-day bankruptcy declaration described an extraordinary failure of corporate controls and reliable financial information.
- Subsequent criminal proceedings established far more than a mere liquidity mismatch: in 2023 SBF was convicted, and DOJ later stated he had misappropriated billions in FTX customer deposits and channeled them to Alameda, among other fraudulent uses.
- Sources: FTX bankruptcy releases; CRS `What Happened at FTX`; Reuters Nov. 2022 and later timeline; DOJ conviction/sentencing releases.

## R3-A3-07 — Historical-knowledge boundary for FTX
- Chapter 11 may use the later-established fraud findings as omniscient historical narration only when clearly framed as facts learned after the collapse.
- Characters in early Nov. 2022 must not speak as though the later criminal verdict was already known.
- FTX≠Alameda Research. Their affiliation and financial relationship are central, but they are distinct entities.

## R3-A3-08 — Ethereum The Merge
- Ethereum's Beacon Chain launched separately on 2020-12-01 as a proof-of-stake consensus chain.
- The Merge was a two-stage mainnet sequence: Bellatrix on the consensus layer and Paris on the execution layer, triggered by terminal total difficulty.
- On 2022-09-15 Ethereum Mainnet completed The Merge, replacing proof-of-work block production with proof-of-stake consensus while preserving the existing execution-layer history/state.
- ethereum.org estimates energy consumption fell by roughly 99.95%.
- The Merge did not itself directly reduce transaction fees or instantly implement sharding/scaling. Do not portray it as a fee-reduction upgrade.
- Sources: Ethereum Foundation 2022-08-12/24 announcements; ethereum.org Merge/Beacon Chain pages.

## R3-A3-09 — Chronology framing
- Terra collapses in May.
- Celsius freezes withdrawals June 13.
- 3AC margin-call/default crisis becomes public in mid/late June and liquidation follows.
- Voyager files Chapter 11 July 5; Celsius files July 13.
- Ethereum completes The Merge Sep 15 amid a damaged market.
- FTX collapses in early Nov and files Chapter 11 Nov 11.
- The novel's final chapter can place The Merge as Ethereum's technical achievement amid the same year's wreckage, then use the later FTX collapse as the final darkness if chapter title/ordering is adjusted, or retain chapter title metaphorically while preserving chronology. In current outline, C11 FTX precedes C12 Merge; to avoid chronological falsification C12 should explicitly return to September as a reflective frame or, preferably, outline order should be corrected to Merge before FTX.

## Act III 창작 경계
- UST algorithmic design must not be generalized to USDC/USDT/DAI.
- Terra/LUNA/UST/Anchor/Terraform Labs are distinct layers/entities.
- Terra collapse amplified market stress but is not a single sufficient cause for every subsequent bankruptcy.
- 3AC, Celsius, Voyager, Genesis, BlockFi, FTX and Alameda are separate actors with different balance sheets and failure mechanisms.
- Customer withdrawal freezes are not the same legal event as bankruptcy filings.
- The Merge preserved Ethereum's transaction history; it did not create a new replacement ETH token for ordinary users.
